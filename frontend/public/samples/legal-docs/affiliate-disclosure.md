## 2. WHAT IS AN AFFILIATE LINK?

An affiliate link is a specially tagged URL that identifies us as the referring source when you visit a merchant's website. Affiliate programs are operated by merchants or affiliate networks, and they pay a referral commission when a qualifying purchase is made through such a link.

Affiliate links on this Site may appear as:

- **"Buy Now" or "Check Price" buttons** on product pages
- **Price comparison links** within comparison tables
- **Product image links** that redirect to a merchant listing

All such links will redirect you to a third-party merchant platform. You are under no obligation to use them.
