## 2. WHAT ARE COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE?

### 2.1 Cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit. Cookies are widely used to make websites work, remember your preferences, and provide information to the site operator about how the site is used.

Cookies can be:

- **First-party cookies** — set by the website you are visiting (in this case, by us).
- **Third-party cookies** — set by a domain other than the one you are visiting (e.g., by an analytics provider or affiliate network).
- **Session cookies** — deleted automatically when you close your browser.
- **Persistent cookies** — remain on your device for a defined duration or until manually deleted.

### 2.2 Browser Local Storage

Local Storage is a browser-based storage mechanism that allows a website to save data **directly within your browser, on your device**. Unlike cookies, data stored in Local Storage:

- Is **not transmitted to our servers** with each page request;
- **Does not expire automatically** (it persists until cleared by you or the site);
- Is accessible only to the domain that created it (this Site).

We use Local Storage — not cookies — to store your preference settings, because it is more appropriate for client-side preferences and does not involve any server-side data transfer.

## 6. YOUR CHOICES & HOW TO MANAGE COOKIES

### 6.1 Cookie Settings on This Site

### 6.2 Browser-Level Cookie Controls

All major browsers allow you to view, block, and delete cookies. Instructions for the most common browsers:

| Browser             | Cookie Settings Location                                        |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Google Chrome**   | Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data   |
| **Mozilla Firefox** | Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data           |
| **Apple Safari**    | Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data                        |
| **Microsoft Edge**  | Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions → Cookies and Site Data |
| **Opera**           | Settings → Advanced → Privacy & Security → Cookies              |

### 6.3 Clearing Local Storage

To clear Local Storage data stored by this Site (including your preference settings):

- **Chrome / Edge / Opera:** DevTools (F12) → Application → Local Storage → Right-click → Clear
- **Firefox:** DevTools (F12) → Storage → Local Storage → Right-click → Delete All
- **Safari:** Develop → Web Inspector → Storage → Local Storage → Delete

Alternatively, clearing all browser site data (cache + cookies + storage) via your browser's Privacy settings will also remove Local Storage entries for this Site.

Note that clearing Local Storage will reset your language, region, and display preferences, and will cause the cookie banner to reappear on your next visit.

### 6.4 Opting Out of Google Analytics

If this Site uses Google Analytics, you can opt out globally using the **[Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)** — available for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera.

### 6.5 Third-Party Opt-Out Tools

To opt out of tracking by advertising and affiliate networks more broadly:

- [Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out](https://optout.aboutads.info/)
