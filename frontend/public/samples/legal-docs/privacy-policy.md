## 3. DATA STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE (LOCAL STORAGE)

To improve your experience and remember your preferences across visits, the Site uses **browser Local Storage** — a standard web technology built into all modern browsers.

### 3.1 What we store in Local Storage

The following preference data may be saved **directly within your browser**, on your device only:

| Data Type                                         | Purpose                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Language preference                               | Display the Site in your selected language            |
| Country / region selection                        | Show region-relevant product data and affiliate links |
| Recent search or browsing history within the Site | Improve navigation and recall recently viewed items   |
| UI/display preferences                            | Maintain your chosen display settings                 |

### 3.2 Key characteristics of Local Storage data

- **It never leaves your device.** Local Storage data is stored in your browser and is not transmitted to, retrieved by, or accessible to our servers under any circumstances.
- **We do not own or control this data** once it is written to your browser. It exists purely to serve your session preferences.
- **It is not PII.** None of this data is associated with your identity, account, or any cross-site profile.
- **You can delete it at any time** by clearing your browser's site data or cache. Instructions vary by browser (Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data, or equivalent).

### 3.3 Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Local Storage used exclusively for functional preference purposes falls under the **"strictly necessary"** or **"functional"** category under ePrivacy and GDPR guidance. It does not require prior consent when used solely to maintain user-selected preferences with no tracking or profiling component.

## 4. AGGREGATED & ANONYMIZED ANALYTICS DATA

When you interact with the Site, certain **non-personal, aggregated, and fully anonymized** data may be collected and transmitted to our servers for the purpose of understanding Site performance and content effectiveness.

### 4.1 What this data includes

- Total number of page views and unique visits (aggregated counts only)
- Total number of clicks on specific products, comparison tables, or affiliate links (aggregated counts only)
- Generalized geographic distribution of traffic (e.g., total volume of visits originating from a given country — a count, not a profile)
- General device category distribution (e.g., percentage of mobile vs. desktop visits — no device fingerprinting)

### 4.2 What this data does NOT include

- Any Personally Identifiable Information
- IP addresses stored in linkage with individual sessions
- Individual browsing paths or behavioral profiles
- Any data that could identify, isolate, or track a specific person or device

### 4.3 Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR)

The processing of strictly aggregated, non-personal analytics data is based on our **legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding how the Site is used in order to maintain and improve it. Because the data is fully anonymized at the point of collection and cannot be reversed to identify any individual, it falls outside the scope of personal data under GDPR Article 4(1) and equivalent definitions.

### 4.4 Data retention

Aggregated analytics data is retained for a maximum of **[e.g., 26 months]** from the date of collection, after which it is deleted or further aggregated into long-term statistical summaries that contain no time-linked granularity.
